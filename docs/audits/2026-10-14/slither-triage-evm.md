## Shared EVM: grouped Low / informational results

- **9 `calls-loop` Low**: eight policy-registration/validation call sites across `initialize` and `setPolicies`, plus the `factory.isVault` lookup in distribution. Policy length and lifetime ID count are capped at 16 (`GameplayVault.sol:82,98`); payouts are capped at 200 (`:251`). Policy changes are authorized/delayed, targets are registered/pinned, and rejecting one policy or recipient rolls back atomically. This is bounded work rather than a public grow-without-limit DoS. Reverting recipients can reject a selected batch; authorizer-selected fresh batches can exclude them. No permanent all-project lock was demonstrated.
- **3 `reentrancy-events` Low**: `Acquired`, `Paid` and `Burned` follow external operations. This correctly emits completion events only after actual success. Guarded Claim and the active phase protocol prevent duplicate successful execution; nested unrelated events are not used as on-chain authorization or accounting inputs.
- **4 `timestamp` Low**: queue-ready sentinels, 48-hour activation comparisons and bounded 300-second Claim/swap deadlines. These are intentional elapsed-time/deadline checks; no timestamp equality or randomness dependency controls funds. Validator clock tolerance near a deadline is an ordinary chain assumption.
- **2 Informational**: `_setPolicies` cyclomatic complexity reflects its boundary checks; the native low-level call checks success and exact outgoing balance. Neither is a demonstrated exploit.
- **3 Optimization**: caching `ids.length` may save gas; `ids` is capped at 16. No security impact.

## Platform NFT: grouped Low / informational results

- **2 `calls-loop` Low**: NFT ownership lookup in stake and transfer in unstake. Each call caps its input at `MAX_BATCH = 32`; the collection is the immutable factory-owned `PlatformNft`. Unstake removes records and checkpoints before outgoing receiver callbacks, and the function is nonreentrant. A rejecting recipient affects its own caller-selected batch and can be replaced; it cannot force a permanent global lock.
- **6 `timestamp` Low**: elapsed quality-seconds, epoch boundaries, same-timestamp checkpoint coalescing, 48-hour mint and resume checks. These are intended time calculations. Same-timestamp checkpoints preserve the integral and only replace the new weight; no synthetic past participation is created. Existing tests cover same-time restake, historic rewards, exact timelock readiness and closed epochs.
- **2 `costly-loop` Informational**: deleting unstaked token mappings is necessary custody cleanup and is bounded to 32 IDs.
- **2 `low-level-calls` Informational**: Vault native sends check `ok` and run after accounting changes under `nonReentrant`; factory forwarding uses the immutable native-income Vault and requires success. No arbitrary target/calldata capability is exposed.
