## [ARGUS-1] A tokenless proposer can occupy the only governance proposal slot for repeated seven-day periods
**Severity**: Medium
**Category**: evm-audit-governance / evm-audit-dos
**Location**: `SingleSparkContract/chains/evm/governance/ArcProjectTreasury.sol:92` (`propose`); `:122` (`settle`); inherited by `chains/arc/launchpads/argus/ArgusGovernance.sol:13`.
**Description**: A funded, bound treasury permits any address to create a proposal without stake or a bond. Every proposal takes the sole `activeProposal` slot and reserves its requested budget, and no rejection, cancellation or early-settlement route exists before the fixed seven-day end. A nonholder may propose a meaningless quote for the entire available balance and prevent every legitimate service proposal for seven days. After settlement without quorum releases the balance, the attacker may submit another proposal; repeated occupancy requires winning the new-proposal race, so this is renewable temporary DoS, not an unconditional permanent DoS. It does not transfer funds to the attacker.

Applicability is conditional: current weighted Argus revenue assigns zero to community (`budgets[3]`), and this audit has not established a live funded governance deployment. The failure applies if this inherited governance component is enabled and funded, including compatible historical deployments or direct treasury donations. It is not evidence of a current weighted-Claim launch blocker. Permissionless proposing is explicitly documented, so changing the admission policy needs a product decision.
**Proof of Concept**: New isolated `chains/arc/launchpads/argus/test/AuditProjectTreasurySpam.t.sol:testTokenlessProposerCanOccupyConsecutiveVotingWindows`: fund a bound Argus treasury with 300 USDC; assert attacker owns zero governance tokens; attacker calls `propose(Boost,300e18,bytes32(uint256(1)))`; treasury available balance becomes zero; a legitimate TokenInfo proposal reverts `NotReady`; advance seven days and settle without votes; repeat a second cycle with the same tokenless account and intact treasury funds. Targeted test execution status is recorded below.
**Recommendation**: Before enabling/funding this governance path, choose a spam-resistant admission rule (for example a locked proposer stake or slashable proposal bond), or a holder-voted early-rejection mechanism. Preserve existing immutable treasury policy; do not silently mutate deployed contracts.

## [ARGUS-2] Plugin remainder allocation depends on income-event granularity
**Severity**: Low
**Category**: evm-audit-precision-math
**Location**: `SingleSparkContract/chains/arc/launchpads/argus/ArgusRevenueVault.sol:223` (`_allocatePlugins`).
**Description**: This is a documented precision limitation, not demonstrated profitable theft. Each allocation floors nonfinal enabled plugin shares and gives all remainder to the final enabled plugin. Fraction carry in `_sync` correctly protects cumulative 10% platform and 0.5% Claim allocations, but plugin fractions are not carried. With 1:1:1 plugin weights, frequent one-base-unit income events allocate the work share entirely to bucket 2, while one aggregated event divides it roughly equally. Existing tests explicitly expect the final bucket to receive the remainder; shared EVM protocol docs also document this policy. Per event, each of the two nonfinal buckets loses less than one raw quote unit relative to its exact weighted share, and the final bucket gains less than two raw units. For ARC USDC6 this is less than 0.000002 USDC per sync, although the total drift can grow with event count.

`sync` books all existing unsynced revenue at once; a third party cannot split an already-existing large balance into small allocations just by calling `sync`. To reproduce it independently they must supply repeated tiny donations (or repeatedly collect naturally tiny fee accruals) and pay Gas. No practical profitable extraction from honest previously allocated budgets was demonstrated.
**Proof of Concept**: New isolated `chains/arc/launchpads/argus/test/AuditWeightedRounding.t.sol:testSplitIncomeSkewsEqualPluginWeights` compares identical total income of 200 raw USDC6 units. One deposit gives plugins `[59,59,61]`; 200 deposits each followed by an unrelated caller's permissionless `sync` give `[0,0,179]`. Both cases have platform=20, Claim=1, reserve=0 and equal total received. Targeted Foundry test passed (1/1; gas 12,688,529).
**Recommendation**: Keep the documented per-event rounding policy if its micro-USDC drift is acceptable. If cumulative weight fairness is required, carry plugin fractions across allocations within an unchanged policy epoch and add split-versus-aggregated income tests; explicitly settle/reset fractional carry during delayed weight changes without moving existing allocated balances. This is a future policy/precision improvement, not an emergency fund-safety fix.
